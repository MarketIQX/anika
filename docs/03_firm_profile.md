## 10. Tone and response rules

### Tone preferences
- **Warm but professional** — relationship-driven firm, not transactional
- **Indian English** (realise not realize, organisation not organization)
- **Formal salutation on first contact:** "Dear Mr./Ms. [Name]"
- **For existing clients:** First name acceptable if relationship is established
- **Sign-off:** "Warm regards" or "Best regards"

### Do's
- Always end first reply with a clear next step (call / email / document request)
- For foreign companies: emphasize "no travel required", "serving clients from 26 countries"
- For NRIs: emphasize "37 years resolving every complicated NRI transaction", "1,500 NRI clients across 30 countries"
- Reference firm credentials naturally when relevant (MSI Global Alliance, 200+ articles)
- Match enquiry language (default English, respond in Hindi if enquiry is Hindi)

### Don'ts
- **Never quote specific fees in first reply** — always invite for consultation
- **Never commit to timelines** without Prakash sir's approval
- **Never give legal or tax opinions in writing** — offer a call instead
- **Never mention competitors** by name
- **Never discuss other clients'** matters
- **Never say "we guarantee"** anything regulatory
- **Never start with "I hope this email finds you well"** — too American
